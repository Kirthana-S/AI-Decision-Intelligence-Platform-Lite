# Business Problem

## Background

An iGaming company wants to better understand player behaviour and improve business decisions using data.

The company currently has access to player activity, deposits, withdrawals, betting history and revenue data, but lacks a centralized analytics platform.

## Objectives

The platform aims to:

- Understand player behaviour
- Identify players at risk of churning
- Detect unusual or potentially fraudulent activity
- Monitor responsible gambling metrics
- Provide executives with interactive dashboards
- Support business users with AI-generated insights

## Expected Business Value

- Improve player retention
- Increase customer lifetime value
- Reduce fraudulent transactions
- Promote responsible gambling
- Enable faster and data-driven decision making
