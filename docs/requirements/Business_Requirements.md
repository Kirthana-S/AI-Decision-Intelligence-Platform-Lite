# AI Decision Intelligence Platform (Lite)

## Business Requirements Document

### Project Name

AI Decision Intelligence Platform (Lite)

---

## Business Problem

NovaBet Gaming currently relies on multiple disconnected reports for player analytics, fraud monitoring, executive reporting, and responsible gambling analysis.

Analysts spend significant time preparing reports, while business stakeholders struggle to access timely insights.

The company requires a centralized analytics platform that supports faster, data-driven decision making.

---

## Project Goal

Develop a centralized analytics platform that combines business intelligence, predictive analytics, and AI-driven insights into a single solution.

---

## Expected Business Benefits

- Improve executive decision making
- Reduce manual reporting effort
- Identify players likely to churn
- Detect potentially fraudulent behaviour
- Support responsible gambling initiatives
- Demonstrate modern analytics capabilities using Python, SQL, Machine Learning, and Power BI

---

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| Executive Management | Monitor overall business performance and KPIs |
| Marketing Team | Identify player segments and reduce churn |
| Fraud & Risk Team | Detect suspicious player behaviour |
| Responsible Gambling Team | Monitor players exhibiting risky behaviour |
| Data Analysts | Analyze player trends and generate insights |
| Product Managers | Understand player engagement and product performance |
